# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone should be able to take part in this project without harassment,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, or how they work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and considerate about the person.
- Critique code and decisions, not people. "This drops writes under
  concurrency" is useful. "This is careless" is not.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before concluding they
  did not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and that a contributor may
  disagree with a review. Both are normal.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Insults, or demeaning comments about a person or a group.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing a vulnerability, a backdoor, or code that removes a
  safety property described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Scope

This applies in every project space, including issues, pull requests, commits,
discussions, and any place where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a concern to **achar.pranav@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
Include what happened, where, and when. You do not need to have a resolution in
mind to report something.

The maintainers will review every report and respond in a way that fits what
happened, which may include a private warning, removal of content, or a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. Whoever is reported does not
take part in deciding the outcome.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted from the ideas in the
