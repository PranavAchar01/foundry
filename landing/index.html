The holding company with no employees.

Foundry doesn't run a business. It runs a portfolio of them — generating hypotheses, shipping real products with real checkout, reading the P&L, and killing the losers. Capital gets reallocated every cycle. Nobody signs off.

See the live portfolio How the loop works

A zero-human company has zero employees — not zero humans.

Labor becomes a variable cost with a spot price. When Foundry's confidence on a decision drops below threshold, it buys fifteen minutes of human expertise through Terac — and that expense posts to the P&L as COGS, on the same ledger the revenue lands on. Expertise is a line item, not a headcount.

The loop

Hypothesis → capital → verdict.

One cycle, running on a cron, writing every decision to an append-only log with its actual reasoning attached.

01

Hypothesize

Generates a business thesis with a named customer and a price.

02

Spawn

Builds the product, deploys it, wires live Stripe checkout to a real URL.

03

Measure

Traffic, conversions, CAC — posted to a single shared ledger.

04

Escalate

Under-confident? Buys human expertise via Terac. Books it as COGS.

05

Allocate

Kills what's dead. Moves the freed capital into what's converting.

Governance

What stops it torching the card.

Enforced in code, with a test that proves the breaker actually halts spend. This is the corporate governance layer of a company with no people in it.

BUDGET_CEILING

Hard per-business and portfolio-wide caps read from env. Exceeding either aborts the cycle before any charge.

CIRCUIT_BREAKER

Anomalous burn rate trips a global halt. Every pending action unwinds; nothing retries on its own.

HUMAN_ESCALATION

Irreversible or legally-exposed decisions cannot execute agent-only. They route to a paid expert first.

APPEND_ONLY_LOG

Every decision, spend, and escalation is written immutably. The agent cannot revise its own history.
