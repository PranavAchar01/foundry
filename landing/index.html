Foundry doesn't run a business. It runs a portfolio of them — generating hypotheses, shipping real products with real checkout, measuring what happens, and killing the ones that go nowhere. Every cycle it decides again. Nobody signs off.

A zero-human company has zero employees — not zero humans.

Labor becomes something the company can buy by the task. When Foundry's confidence on a decision drops below threshold, it posts the work to Terac and a human picks it up. Expertise is a line item, not a headcount.

The loop

Hypothesis → capital → verdict.

One cycle, running on a cron, writing every decision to an append-only log with its actual reasoning attached.

01

Hypothesize

Generates a business thesis with a named customer and a price.

02

Spawn

Builds the product, deploys it, wires live Stripe checkout to a real URL.

03

Measure

Traffic and conversions, posted to a single shared ledger.

04

Escalate

Under-confident? Posts the work to Terac and a human picks it up.

05

Allocate

Kills what's dead. Puts the freed effort into what's converting.

Governance

What stops it torching the card.

Enforced in code, with a test that proves the breaker actually halts the system. This is the corporate governance layer of a company with no people in it.

BUDGET_CEILING

Hard per-business and portfolio-wide caps read from env. Exceeding either aborts the cycle before any charge.

CIRCUIT_BREAKER

Anomalous burn rate trips a global halt. Every pending action unwinds; nothing retries on its own.

HUMAN_ESCALATION

Irreversible or legally-exposed decisions cannot execute agent-only. They route to a paid expert first.

APPEND_ONLY_LOG

Every decision and escalation is written immutably. The agent cannot revise its own history.
